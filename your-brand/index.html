FAQ

Good questions.

Do I need any technical skills?

No. We set up and run everything: domain, email, hosting and the app. You send us your brand and start coaching.

Can I use a domain I already own?

Yes. We connect it for you. If you don't have one yet, you can start on a free yourbrand.wildmethod.app subdomain.

Will my clients see Wild Method or Silvestre?

No. Your clients see your name, logo and colors across the app.

What's the difference between the web app and the store app?

On the Monthly plan your clients use a web app they add to their home screen. From the Quarterly plan, you also get your own app on the App Store and Google Play, listed with your name, icon and brand.

Is it only for personal trainers?

No. It's built for personal trainers and nutritionists. Each professional gets their own panel, so your team can be trainers, nutritionists, or both.

How do I get paid by my clients?

Payments are part of your admin panel. You connect your own Stripe account, clients pay by card and the money goes straight to you. Invoicing and taxes live in the same panel.

I'm a freelancer. Does it still work?

Yes. It works whether you train under your own name or run a company registered in a UAE free zone.

Do you manage my social media?

No. We create a launch set of posts and, with any Annual plan, film and edit content for you. Posting stays with you.

Where are you based?

We're based in Dubai, UAE, and work with personal trainers remotely.
